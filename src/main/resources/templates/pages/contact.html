Common Questions

Booking Process

Once you submit a booking request, the photographer is notified immediately. They will review your event details and package selection, then accept or decline based on availability.

Payments & Refunds

Payments are processed securely via our gateway only after a booking is confirmed by the photographer. If a booking is declined, no charges are applied.

Photographer Profiles

You can view comprehensive photographer profiles, including their bio, years of experience, portfolio images, and available pricing packages before making a request.

Reviews & Ratings

After your event, you can leave a review and rating for your photographer. These are visible on their profile to help future clients make informed decisions.
